HOW TO READ THIS

SELL HIGHER covered call

You hold SOL and pick a price above spot.

triggers
your SOL is sold at that price.

BUY LOWER cash-secured put

You hold USDC and pick a price below spot.

triggers
you buy SOL at that price.
doesn't
you keep your USDC.

CHANCE OF TRIGGERING

The market's rough odds that SOL is past your price on expiry day. The further a price is from spot, the lower the odds, and the smaller the premium.

Not a limit order: only the price on expiry day counts. Touching it earlier does nothing.

EITHER WAY

You keep the premium you were paid upfront.
